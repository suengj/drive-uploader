Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Drive Uploader is used only by its owner.

  • Data accessed. It accesses the owner's Google Drive (scope: drive), and only to create and update files that the owner produces.
  • Use of Google user data. Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI models.
  • Credentials. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's server, with restricted permissions. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Drive Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
